Pint's Disenchant - Changelog

v1.2.4
- New Soulbound filter next to Bind on Equip. Untick it to keep soulbound items off the list; untick Bind on Equip
  to keep BoE items off. Tick both to see everything (the old behaviour).

v1.2.3
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item, spell and profession functions where the old ones are gone.

v1.2.2
- Fixed "That item is not a valid target" when disenchanting quickly: an item that has just been disenchanted
  stays in your bag for a moment, and the next click could aim at it. It's now skipped until it's gone.
- A click that comes too soon can no longer equip the item instead of disenchanting it: the buttons now cast
  Disenchant and target the item in one step, and do nothing if the cast can't start.

v1.2.1
- Fixed a red error appearing now and then while disenchanting quickly: a targeting cursor left over from a
  click that didn't finish is now cleared before the next disenchant.
- /pd log now lists any error the game shows right after a click, with what was happening at the time.

v1.2
- First public release.
- A list of every item in your bags you can disenchant, with one "Disenchant next" button and a keybind.
- Choose which qualities to include (Uncommon, Rare, Epic) and whether Bind on Equip items are included.
- Items your Enchanting skill can't handle yet are marked with the skill they need.
- Right-click an item to ignore it; items the game refuses twice are ignored for you.
- Clicks made while the last disenchant is still finishing are skipped instead of failing.
- With Auctionator: auction value shown next to Bind on Equip items, so you don't disenchant something worth selling.
