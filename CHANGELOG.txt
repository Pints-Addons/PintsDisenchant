Pint's Disenchant - Changelog

v1.2
- First public release.
- A list of every item in your bags you can disenchant, with one "Disenchant next" button and a keybind.
- Choose which qualities to include (Uncommon, Rare, Epic) and whether Bind on Equip items are included.
- Items your Enchanting skill can't handle yet are marked with the skill they need.
- Right-click an item to ignore it; items the game refuses twice are ignored for you.
- Clicks made while the last disenchant is still finishing are skipped instead of failing.
- With Auctionator: auction value shown next to Bind on Equip items, so you don't disenchant something worth selling.
